# 3.0.9

Fixes for the course page under a colour scheme, mainly on Moodle 5.3.

- Course section summaries take the scheme's text colour. Before, they kept the theme's grey, which had too little
  contrast on some schemes, such as Cream.
- On Moodle 5.3, no light corners show behind the rounded course section cards.
- On Moodle 5.3, the course tab bar sits on the scheme's page colour, so no lighter band shows behind the tabs.
